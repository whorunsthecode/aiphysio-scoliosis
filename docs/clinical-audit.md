## Auditor headlines

**PSSE-certified physiotherapist.** The engine's convex/concave derivation is correct, but the library then applies it inconsistently: the sitting waist fold is cued INTO the curve for thoracic patients, child's pose has an inverted rationale, "frog in the pond" is an unsourced single-patient exercise never mirrored for left curves, and side-plank-wrong-side is called an ABSOLUTE contraindication on the strength of one uncontrolled yoga case series while the product's own outcome measure requires bilateral side bridges. Nothing in the library is actually a Schroth or SEAS exercise; the PSSE core (pelvic corrections, elongation, 3D autocorrection, stabilisation, ADL integration) is absent, so the "Schroth" labelling is exposure, not substance.

**Spine surgeon (O&T).** The engineering discipline is good (red-flag tiers, MDC gating, honest demotion of the webcam scan), but the clinical layer has one flatly backwards corrective cue, a single-point-of-failure way of learning convexity (self-reported "bulge", or an LLM reading an X-ray photo), a red-flag screen that misses the scoliosis-specific referrals (neural-axis soft signs, post-fusion, congenital/NF1, skeletal-maturity/bracing window, stenosis), and "cascade" chains that have no clinical basis and imply progression detection from a sensor the team already knows cannot detect it.

**Clinical safety / informatics.** The red-flag engine is well-designed as a pure function, but it is wired to a screen that runs once and never again, its "urgent" tier does nothing, its adolescent rule can never fire because age is never collected, and the LLM safety floor is stripped from the prompt before the user-facing reply is written. Population gating (post-fusion, braced, osteoporotic, pregnant, Marfan/EDS) is absent, and a single wrong left/right self-report silently converts every asymmetric cue into the product's own declared contraindication.

**Clinical biomechanist.** The ATR inclinometer is the right instrument and its maths is mostly sound, but its sign convention is undefined, its "roll" check measures the wrong thing, and its screening bands are meaningless for the diagnosed adults it serves; the outcome module's MDC identities are correct but gated on far too few pairs and never wired to the side-bridge trend; three of the five webcam form checks (and all surviving mm posture bands) sit at or below single-camera landmark error.

**Guidelines & evidence (SOSORT).** Balance has a genuinely good safety scaffold (red-flag gate, unknown-side withholding, honest ATR/MDC plumbing) but its exercise content overreaches its evidence: one Tier-3 side cue is anatomically reversed, the side-plank "contraindication" rests on a case series that failed replication, the product never tells a growing patient with Cobb >=20-25 deg that exercise is not a substitute for bracing, its ATR "refer at 7 deg" contradicts the HK school pathway it targets, and it is currently nowhere near the Yuan 2025 digital-PSSE comparator it is implicitly measured against.

#### `waist-fold-cued-into-curve` — library.ts — sitting_waist_fold (Tier 3)  
_Lens: PSSE-certified physiotherapist · raised as critical · confidence high_

**Product currently:** asymmetric_cues.right_thoracic: "Fold to the left to open the right concave thoracic side." and left_thoracic: "Fold to the right to open the left concave thoracic side." Description: "fold left waist (compresses left side, opens right)."

**Problem:** A right thoracic curve is convex right and concave LEFT (profile.ts derives this correctly, and schroth_rotational_breathing in the same file says so: "Inhale into the LEFT ribs to open the concave side"). The waist-fold cue labels the RIGHT side as concave, then tells the patient to side-bend LEFT — which compresses the already-shortened left concave ribs and opens the right convexity. That is lateral flexion in the direction of the deformity: the exercise reinforces the curve for every thoracic pattern, and the two Tier-3/Tier-5 items contradict each other about which side is concave. The most likely origin is that the cue was transcribed from one patient's programme where "fold left" was a LUMBAR correction (left lumbar curve in a right-thoracic/left-lumbar S: side-bending left toward the lumbar convexity opens the right lumbar concavity) and then mis-keyed to the thoracic curve.

**Correction:** Fix the labels and direction now: for right_thoracic the corrective lateral flexion is to the RIGHT (toward the convexity, opening the left concave side); mirror for left_thoracic. If the intent was a lumbar correction, key it on lumbar patterns (left_lumbar → fold left; right_lumbar → fold right; double_RT_LL → fold left labelled as lumbar) and drop the thoracic keys. Better still, replace a pure side-bend with the Schroth/SEAS form: elongation first, then a lateral TRANSLATION of the thoracic block toward the concave side with a concave-side breath, and never let "open the convex side" appear anywhere. Add a unit test that asserts, for every exercise, that any cue containing "concave" agrees with deriveRegionalSides(); this contradiction would have been caught mechanically.

**Evidence:** Convention: curve named by side of convexity (SRS glossary; Lehnert-Schroth, Three-Dimensional Treatment for Scoliosis). Schroth corrections always open the concavity and never side-bend toward the concave side; SEAS active self-correction likewise moves the trunk away from the deformity (Berdishevsky et al., Scoliosis Spinal Disord 2016, seven-schools review). The internal inconsistency is verifiable directly in library.ts lines 224–233 vs 361–362.

**Refuters' notes:** Direction and label are both wrong, and the same file contradicts itself about which side is concave. The suggested unit test (cue text containing 'concave' must agree with deriveRegionalSides) is a good, cheap mechanical guard. · The fix is a two-line label/direction swap plus a test; the auditor's suggested 'translation rather than side-bend' rewrite is nice-to-have, not required to close the finding.


#### `ongoing-screen-never-runs` — lib/safety/redFlags.ts questionsFor('ongoing'); app/session/page.tsx:100-127; components/onboarding/steps/SafetyStep.tsx  
_Lens: Clinical safety / informatics · raised as critical · confidence high_

**Product currently:** redFlags.ts defines 7 questions with askAt 'ongoing', and session/page.tsx comments: 'Re-run the stored screen every session rather than trusting the onboarding verdict indefinitely — the ruleset changes, and so do symptoms.'

**Problem:** The ongoing question set is dead code: questionsFor('ongoing') is referenced only in scripts/check-safety.ts. The session page re-runs triage() against profile.safetyScreen, i.e. the same onboarding answers, so symptoms cannot change in either direction. (a) A user who answered 'no' to bladder/bowel change at onboarding and develops it three weeks later is never asked again; the gate is blind to the one scenario it exists for. (b) A user who answered 'yes' (e.g. saddle numbness) is blocked on every session indefinitely, with no path to record 'I was seen and cleared' except re-running the whole onboarding and changing the answer — which both trains users to flip a safety answer to get past a wall and destroys the record that the flag ever fired. (c) The Telegram flag_safety tool writes only an agent_observations row; it never touches safetyScreen, so an emergency symptom disclosed in chat leaves the exercise gate fully open. Answers are a flat Record<string, boolean> with a single profile-level safety_screened_at, so there is no per-answer timestamp and no history.

**Correction:** 1) Render the 7-item ongoing set as a forced step in the session flow (before pain_check) whenever safety_screened_at is older than a fixed interval — recommend 7 days for the 3 emergency-tier items (bladder/bowel, saddle numbness, progressive weakness; three taps) and 28 days for the rest — plus a one-tap 'anything new since last time?' gate on every session that expands into the emergency 3 if 'yes'. 2) Store answers as a timestamped append-only log (question_id, value, answered_at, source: onboarding|session|chat) and have triage() use the latest value per question. 3) An emergency 'yes' must be cleared only by an explicit 'I have been assessed by a clinician' acknowledgement with a date, recorded as its own event; never by editing the original answer. 4) Have toolFlagSafety, for categories mapping to emergency rules (numbness_or_tingling with saddle/groin words, weakness, bladder/bowel), also write the corresponding screening answer so the next session is gated. 5) Add tests: 'yes at onboarding then cleared then no at session' and 'no at onboarding, yes at session' both produce the expected gate state.

**Evidence:** Code inspection of the four files named. Clinical reasoning: cauda equina and progressive motor deficit are time-critical and typically arise after enrolment, not at it; a one-time screen has near-zero sensitivity for incident symptoms. ISO 14971 hazard: 'exercise delivered during undetected emergency-tier symptoms' — the current control (one-time onboarding screen) does not mitigate the identified sequence of events.

**Refuters' notes:** Keep the per-session single-tap gate expanding to the three emergency items; treat the 7/28-day intervals as proposals to confirm with the supervising clinician, not as evidence-backed values. The append-only log with source and the clinician-clearance event are the right data model. · The blocked-forever path (b) is the part most likely to cause a real bad outcome: users learn that safety answers are a wall to get past. Prioritise the 'I have been assessed' clearance event and the 3-item weekly re-ask; the full 7-item 28-day cadence can follow.

**Evidence:** Code inspection of conversation.ts lines 187-267 and tools.ts 423-442. **Refuters' notes:** All four failure modes are real. #### `waist-fold-backwards` — lib/exercises/library.ts — sitting_waist_fold, childs_pose_side_reach (concave/convex convention)  
_Lens: Spine surgeon (O&T) · raised as critical · confidence high_

**Product currently:** sitting_waist_fold: right_thoracic -> "Fold to the left to open the right concave thoracic side"; left_thoracic -> "Fold to the right to open the left concave thoracic side". Description hard-codes "fold left waist (compresses left side, opens right)" on an 'any'-pattern exercise. childs_pose_side_reach: "Reach toward convex thoracic side to LENGTHEN it… right_thoracic -> longer reach right (convex thoracic side)".

**Problem:** Definitive convention: a curve is named for the side of its CONVEXITY. A right thoracic curve is convex right, so the concave (collapsed, short) side is LEFT — which is exactly what schroth_rotational_breathing and bird_dog_asymmetric_hold already say. sitting_waist_fold says the opposite (concave = right) AND its movement is backwards: by its own description folding left compresses the left side, i.e. it closes the concavity of a right thoracic curve and exaggerates the deformity — the one thing a corrective side-bend must never do. This is the founder's own pattern (RT + LL demo profile) leaking in: fold-left IS corrective for the LEFT LUMBAR component (convex left, concave right), but the cue is keyed to right_thoracic and labelled thoracic. childs_pose_side_reach moves in the right direction by accident but its rationale is inverted — the convex side is already the long side; nobody lengthens the convexity. Every side-keyed cue audited: CORRECT — schroth_rotational_breathing (RT -> inhale left), bird_dog_asymmetric_hold (RT -> left arm/right leg, 'opens left concave'), side_plank_convex_thoracic_side_down (RT -> right down), frog_in_the_pond (pattern-gated). BACKWARDS — sitting_waist_fold (both cues + description). WRONG RATIONALE, RIGHT DIRECTION — childs_pose_side_reach. UNGROUNDED, NOT DEMONSTRABLY WRONG — hip_bridge_pelvic_press_down / single_leg_bridge_variant 'press convex-lumbar pelvis down' (no PSSE school I know cues this; leave to the physio).

**Correction:** Adopt one rule everywhere and encode it as a helper (foldToward(curve) = convexSide(curve)): 'A corrective lateral fold goes TOWARD the convexity of the curve being targeted, so the concavity opens.' Then: sitting_waist_fold right_thoracic -> 'Fold to the RIGHT so the LEFT (concave) thoracic side lengthens'; left_thoracic -> fold LEFT. Because a waist fold acts mostly on the lumbar/thoracolumbar segment, add lumbar keys (left_lumbar -> fold left, right_lumbar -> fold right) and for double curves drive it from the lumbar convexity, not the thoracic, with a note that the thoracic must be held stable (or drop the exercise from self-guided for S-curves). Delete the hard-coded 'fold left' description. childs_pose_side_reach: keep the direction, rewrite rationale to 'reach toward the convex side so the concave side lengthens'. Add a unit test that asserts, for every asymmetric_cues entry, that any string containing 'concave' names opposite(convexSide) and any containing 'convex' names convexSide — that test would have caught this. Soften side-plank 'wrong side is a contraindication' to 'not the cued side' (see contraindications finding).

**Evidence:** Naming convention (curve named by convexity; concavity is the short, rib-collapsed side) is universal in scoliosis texts and SRS terminology. Schroth/PSSE corrective side-bending elongates the concavity (Lehnert-Schroth; Weiss). Internal evidence: the codebase's own profile.ts line 28 states 'Concave side = opposite of lean', so the waist-fold cue contradicts the product's own model. Fishman 2014 side plank convex-side-down — UNVERIFIED from memory but consistent with the library.

**Refuters' notes:** Add the pattern-wide 'concave = opposite(convexSide)' unit test the finder proposes; it would catch both this and any future copy drift. · Side-keyed cue audit is accurate: breathing, bird dog, side plank and frog are consistent; only the waist fold is backwards and the child's pose rationale is inverted. Hip-bridge 'press convex-lumbar pelvis down' is indeed not a recognised PSSE cue but is harmless — leave it as physio-configurable.

#### `side-plank-absolute-contra` — contraindications.ts checkSidePlankSide (category: "absolute") + library side_plank_convex_thoracic_side_down + selectProgram physio_cleared side check  
_Lens: PSSE-certified physiotherapist · raised as critical · confidence high_

**Product currently:** "Side plank should be done with your [convex] side down… Holding the other side reinforces the curve." Wrong-side side plank is an ABSOLUTE contraindication; library description says "Wrong side held long is a contraindication."

**Problem:** Convex-side-down side plank is the Fishman yoga protocol, not PSSE. Its evidence is a single uncontrolled serial case series (25 patients, mixed idiopathic and degenerative, self-selected compliance, radiographs read by the authors, mean ~1.5 min/day) reporting Cobb improvement; it has never been replicated in a controlled trial, and no study shows that the opposite-side plank worsens a curve. Mainstream PSSE (Schroth, SEAS, BSPTS) does not prescribe the unilateral plank as a corrective and most Schroth clinicians would question the rationale (the concave-side trunk musculature is the shortened/underactive side). Calling the non-convex side an ABSOLUTE contraindication (same tier as inversions) is indefensible clinically — and it contradicts the product's own outcomes module, which measures McGill side-bridge endurance LEFT and RIGHT and calls a ratio: the patient cannot perform that test without doing the "contraindicated" side. #### `mdc-min-pairs-inadequate` — lib/pose/repeatability.ts MIN_PAIRS_FOR_MDC=5, computeRepeatability(); used by atrPairsFrom and enduranceMdc  
_Lens: Clinical biomechanist · raised as major · confidence high_

**Product currently:** SEM = SD_diff/√2; MDC95 = 1.96·SD_diff; LoA = bias ± 1.96·SD_diff; "needs ≥5 pairs"; posture/endurance trends gated on the resulting MDC.

**Problem:** The identities are correct. The gate is not. With 5 pairs (4 df) the 95% CI on the SD spans roughly 0.60× to 2.9× the point estimate (chi-square: √(4/11.14) to √(4/0.484)), so the MDC can be under-estimated by ~40% or over-estimated ~3-fold — i.e. the gate will call noise 'real change' or hide real change, with a confident-sounding one-decimal number attached. Even 10 pairs gives ~0.69×–1.83×. Further: (a) the bias term is computed but never tested — a significant bias (fatigue, learning) means the pairs are not exchangeable and SD_diff/√2 understates SEM; (b) endurance holds are heteroscedastic (error scales with duration) so a raw-seconds MDC is wrong at both ends; (c) no ICC is computed, which is what any reviewer for Spine or Gait & Posture would ask for first, alongside a Bland–Altman plot with the LoA you already compute.

**Correction:** (1) Raise the per-user gate to 10 pairs and, below ~20, multiply by the upper 95% confidence bound of SD_diff (s·√((n−1)/χ²_{0.025,n−1})) so the MDC errs conservative; expose n on the UI line. (2) Pool a population MDC across consenting users (ICC(2,1) with 95% CI, SEM, MDC95, Bland–Altman with proportional-bias check) and use it as the prior, shrinking toward the personal estimate as pairs accumulate. (3) Log-transform endurance seconds before computing repeatability. (4) Test bias with a paired t-test (or require |bias| < SEM) and, if it fails, tell the user the protocol drifted rather than emitting an MDC. (5) Add an ICC(2,1) to the Repeatability type — it costs ten lines.

**Evidence:** Standard test–retest definitions: SEM = SD_diff/√2 (when derived from paired differences), MDC95 = 1.96·√2·SEM (Weir JP, J Strength Cond Res 2005; de Vet et al., Measurement in Medicine, 2011). Chi-square CI on a variance is textbook. COSMIN recommends ≥50 participants for a reliability study — the per-user context is different, but the small-n interval width is not negotiable. High confidence on the statistics.

**Refuters' notes:** Chi-square inflation of the small-n MDC and a bias check are the useful parts; drop the per-user ICC suggestion or scope it to pooled data. · For ATR specifically, two back-to-back readings per session accumulate pairs quickly and the same-session pairs are the right basis for a within-session MDC.

## What is done right — keep it


**PSSE-certified physiotherapist**

- profile.ts derives convexity correctly and in the user's own words ("which way does your back bulge" = convex/lean side; concave = opposite), and deriveRegionalSides handles thoracic and lumbar separately for S-curves. This is the right foundation — the errors are downstream in cue text, not in the model.

- bird_dog_asymmetric_hold assigns the correct arm (concave-side arm, i.e. LEFT arm for right thoracic) for a longer hold; the biomechanics are defensible (ipsilateral lateral-trunk lengthening plus a left-rotation tendency that matches the derotation direction for a right thoracic curve).
- childs_pose_side_reach's ACTION direction (reach right for right thoracic) is correct even though its rationale is inverted — fix the words, keep the movement.

### Prescribe → Verify → Report: the clinician-owned prescription as the product's spine
_Angle: clinician_workflow_

**Flip the product from a patient app that a physio occasionally receives a PDF from into a clinician-prescribed, dose-verified home-delivery instrument: the physio enters the prescription (exercises, sides, dose in minutes/day and days/week, red-flag disposition) in a 5-minute portal, the app delivers exactly that and nothing else, and the physio gets a between-visit ledger of verified minutes vs prescribed, pain/PSFS, apex ATR (MDC-gated) and any safety event — so the physio, not the patient, is the one who insists on it.**

**Why 100x:** Mechanism, not adjectives: (1) Distribution flips from B2C one-at-a-time to one clinician = every patient on their list. A PSSE physio or a scoliosis clinic carries 50-300 active patients; one adopting clinician is worth more than a year of consumer acquisition, and adherence to a clinician-mandated tool is far higher than to a self-downloaded coach. (2) It answers the one question every scoliosis clinician cannot answer today and cares about most: 'did they actually do the 30-45 min/day, and at what dose?' PSSE only shows Cobb effects in trials at a stated home dose (the digest cites Schreiber 2015/2016: 30-45 min/day; Yuan 2025: 30 min/day, 5+ days/week) and home adherence is the known failure point of the whole method; a physio who can see 'prescribed 210 min/week, verified 95, self-reported 160, side plank done 11 of 14 days' changes what they do at the next visit. That is workflow value, which is what makes a clinician write 'use Balance' on the prescription. (3) It structurally deletes the product's largest confirmed clinical-risk class. Nearly every critical finding in the audit (sitting_waist_fold backwards, 'lean side' laterality inversion, X-ray photo convexity flips, absolute-tier folk contraindications, invented cascades, no fusion/brace/maturity handling) exists because the product is guessing the clinician's decisions from lay input. When the physio states the pattern, the sides, the exercises, the dose, the fusion/brace/maturity status and their own contraindication list, the self-guided inference layer becomes a fallback for the unreferred adult, not the core. The clinician's stated convexity overrides self-report; the clinician's exercise list replaces the library's side-keying; the clinician's dose becomes the adherence denominator. (4) It makes the CUHK/trial claim true instead of aspirational: a prescription snapshot plus provenance-flagged adherence events (form_check_verified | timer_only | chat_self_report) is exactly the per-protocol denominator a trial statistician needs, which is the digest's own conclusion about what the 'trial that can finally tell whether the exercise was done' pitch requires. (5) It unlocks a payer: clinics and insurers/pilots pay for remote monitoring and adherence data; patients rarely pay for exercise reminders. The product stops being 'an exercise app for scoliosis' and becomes 'the delivery-and-verification layer for prescribed PSSE', which is a category with a buyer.

**Builds on:** Directly reusable: (a) physio_cleared mode in lib/exercises/selectProgram.ts already treats the physio programme as authoritative; it just needs to become the ONLY path when a clinician is linked, and to consume a structured prescription instead of regex on LLM-normalised names. (b) /api/parse-program + lib/prompts/parseProgram.ts already turns pasted text into a ParsedProgram mapped to library ids: this becomes the clinician's paste-to-prefill step (they confirm, not the patient). (c) The liaison agent + liaison_documents/appointments tables (app/api/agents/liaison/route.ts) already assemble a per-appointment handoff from observations; it becomes a weekly clinician-facing ledger rather than a one-off PDF. (d) lib/agents/context.ts AdherenceSnapshot, lib/session/persist.ts exercises_completed and the per-rep form scores in lib/exercises/formCheck/* give the raw material for a provenance-flagged adherence event; toolFlagSafety and lib/safety/redFlags.ts give the safety-event stream to surface to the clinician. (e) lib/outcomes/compute.ts (PSFS, side-bridge, Sorensen) and lib/pose/repeatability.ts (MDC machinery) plus lib/atr/compute.ts give the outcome columns; atrPairsFrom already collects the pairs the MDC gate needs. (f) app/care-team/page.tsx is a ready scaffold for a clinician dashboard (programs, observations, appointments, documents are already fetched). (g) Supabase auth and the privacy/redaction layer (lib/privacy, agents never see names) extend naturally to a clinician role with patient linking.

**Needs:** Engineering: clinician role + consent-gated patient linking (invite code or QR at the visit); a Prescription object as first-class data (exerciseId or clinician's own free exercise, side enum left|right|both, sets/reps/seconds, minutesPerDay, daysPerWeek, clinician-stated curve pattern and convex sides, fusion/brace/maturity flags, clinician's own do-not-do list, valid-from/until) snapshotted weekly; adherence event schema with provenance and formScore, with weekly verified and reported adherence computed against the snapshot; clinician weekly ledger view + per-patient CSV export; clinician-owned exercise media (their own video or the library's) so Schroth/SEAS-certified physios are not forced into Balance's Tier-3 wording; hard rule that side-keyed library cues are withheld unless clinician-stated (kills the laterality problem); safety-event routing to the linked clinician with the existing condition-free copy; Traditional Chinese for both sides. Clinical: 2-3 design-partner PSSE physios (HK and one Schroth/SEAS-certified clinic) to co-design the 5-minute prescription flow; a clinician sign-off on every remaining self-guided cue (the audit's ask) so the fallback path is not a liability that scares clinicians away from the linked path. Data: fix the denominator first (prescribed dose stored), then instrument verified vs reported minutes; expect form-check-verified to be honest only for side plank sag and rep counting per the biomech audit, so the primary verified signal is timer-on-exercise-screen plus rep count, labelled as such. Partnerships: CUHK orthopaedics/physio (docs/cuhk-approach.md already points here) for a monitoring-arm pilot; a HK physio association or two private scoliosis clinics as the first paying accounts. Regulatory/ethics: once a clinician relies on the ledger it is a clinical decision-support tool; expect PDPO data-processing agreements with clinics, an REB submission for the CUHK arm, and to keep all claims to adherence/pain/function/endurance (never curve change) so it stays in the lowest software-as-medical-device risk tier.

**Biggest risk:** Clinician non-adoption due to friction and liability, producing a two-sided cold start where neither side has data. ### Trial Mode: a locked-prescription, dose-denominated, provenance-tagged adherence instrument with statistician-grade export
_Angle: trial_grade_

**Turn Balance from a coach that prescribes into an instrument that measures: the clinician's PSSE prescription becomes a versioned, signed object; every set is logged as an adherence event with prescribed-vs-completed dose and a source tag (form-check-verified | timer-only | chat-self-report); the LLM coach, self-guided programme generator, cascade models and scan-trend language are switched OFF in this mode; and the whole thing exports as a pseudonymised per-participant table with a data dictionary a trial statistician can drop into a per-protocol analysis.**

**Why 100x:** The multiplier is a change of buyer and a change of what the artefact is worth per user. Today the product's value per user is one patient's exercise habit, and the audit shows that as a prescriber it is currently net-negative (backwards waist-fold cue, invented cascades, a contraindication engine that flags physio programmes and blocks nothing). As a trial instrument, value per user is a fraction of the cost of the trial it makes interpretable: every PSSE RCT (Schreiber-style, 6 months, 30-45 min/day home dose) has the same unsolved confound - if the exercise arm underperforms nobody can say whether the exercise failed or was not done, because adherence is a paper diary. docs/cuhk-approach.md already names this as "the only part of this product genuinely ahead of what a research group could commission" but the code does not deliver it: exercises_completed has no denominator, no timestamps per set, mixes chat self-report with camera-verified reps, and the prescription is never snapshotted. Mechanism, step by step: (1) a locked prescription gives adherence a denominator, so weekly adherence = verified minutes / prescribed minutes exists for the first time; (2) the source tag lets a statistician run per-protocol analysis on verified dose and sensitivity analysis on reported dose - that is the analysis nobody in PSSE research can currently run, and it yields the first dose-response data in the field; (3) switching off every prescribing/coaching component in Trial Mode converts the audit's liabilities into non-issues (the physio's prescription is authoritative, the app adds no co-intervention that would confound the trial arm) and drops the regulatory posture from "software that prescribes exercise" to "software that records exercise"; (4) a trial that designs the instrument into its protocol produces a peer-reviewed paper citing it, which is the validation every clinician demands before recommending it to patients - so the research angle is also the only credible route back to the consumer/clinic market; (5) the same ledger, once a few hundred patient-years exist, is the longitudinal behaviour-plus-pain-plus-ATR dataset the roadmap's TOCI/progression conversation needs. One instrument, three markets (trials, PSSE clinics, then patients), and the trial market pays in credibility rather than cash, which is what this product is actually short of.

**Builds on:** lib/session/persist.ts already writes a Supabase sessions row keyed by profile_id with started_at/completed_at/pain_check/exercises_completed - the ledger is an expansion of exerciseSummaries into per-set events, not a new pipeline. lib/exercises/formCheck/* produce per-rep form scores and compensation events that are computed but never persisted; the rep counters for hip bridge/lunge and the side-plank hold timer are the pieces the biomech audit rates PLAUSIBLE, which is exactly what "verified" should be built on. lib/prompts/parseProgram.ts + selectProgram physio_cleared mode already ingest a pasted physio programme with source_text - that is the prescription object, missing only versioning, sets/reps/minutes-per-day/days-per-week fields, a clinician signature and a lock. lib/pose/repeatability.ts has SEM/MDC95/Bland-Altman and buildMdcTable, and lib/atr atrPairsFrom collects test-retest pairs - the instrument's own reliability study is mostly written. lib/outcomes/compute.ts (PSFS, Sorensen, side-bridge L/R) are the secondary outcomes a trial would want alongside adherence. lib/agents/tools.ts log_exercise already writes to exercises_completed with a source column ("real") - the provenance enum slots straight in. scripts/check-safety.ts is the pattern for a build-time lint that fails Trial Mode if any prescribing/trend copy leaks. lib/safety/redFlags.ts is the adverse-event screen an ethics committee will ask for. docs/cuhk-approach.md is the pitch; the feature makes its one-sentence claim true.

**Needs:** ENGINEERING (8-12 weeks for a shippable v1): adherence event schema {participantId(pseudonymous), exerciseId, prescriptionVersion, startedAt, endedAt, prescribedSets/reps/seconds, completedSets/reps/seconds, source enum, formScore 0-1|null, compensationEvents[], deviceId, appVersion, rulesetHash}; versioned prescription table with clinician id, signature timestamp and lock; Trial Mode feature flag that disables self_guided selection, Coach/Companion nudges, cascade.ts, posture-scan mm/trend output and all curve-change copy; weekly adherence computation with verified and reported figures; CSV/Parquet export + data dictionary + audit log, REDCap-importable; site dashboard for missing-data and device-failure monitoring; raise MIN_PAIRS_FOR_MDC to 10 with CI-adjusted MDC and add ICC(2,1) per the biomech audit; the deterministic safety pre-filter and timestamped safety-answer log from the safety_informatics findings (an ethics committee will require the adverse-event pathway to work). CLINICAL: one PSSE-certified physiotherapist to own the prescription editor and sign off any cue text that remains visible (Tier-3 content must be fixed or removed before any patient sees it in a trial); a supervised-observation validation study (n~20-30, therapist-observed home sessions vs app-logged verified minutes; home-helper phone-ATR test-retest vs clinician scoliometer) - this is the paper that makes the instrument citable. DATA/GOVERNANCE: pseudonymisation at the edge, HK PDPO compliance, CUHK-NTEC CREC ethics submission, data-ownership terms conceded to the study upfront (as cuhk-approach.md recommends), parental consent and child assent in Traditional Chinese and English for any under-18 arm, guardian/clinician gating for minors. PARTNERSHIPS: a CUHK O&T researcher planning the successor to NCT03427970 (or any SOSORT-affiliated PSSE group - the instrument is trial-agnostic), a PSSE clinic for the validation pilot, and a biostatistician to co-author the data dictionary so the export is built to the analysis, not the other way round. Resolve HSBC IP and tag the repo first, per the existing checklist.

**Biggest risk:** That "verified" is not verification. The biomech audit rates most single-webcam checks as theatre (hip-height cues, spine rotation, press-down side), and what survives - rep counts, hold timers, side-plank sag - can confirm a body moved in roughly the right way for roughly the right time, not that the prescribed corrective was performed with the prescribed asymmetry or effort. If the validation study shows verified minutes disagree materially with therapist-observed sessions (wrong exercise counted, half-effort holds counted, two-person or off-camera sessions not counted), the instrument collapses to a timestamped diary and the whole premise of the pitch fails in front of the one audience that will check. Mitigation is to define "verified" narrowly and honestly (presence + matched movement pattern + duration, with formScore reported separately and never inflated), publish the agreement statistics before making the claim, keep timer_only and self_report as first-class categories rather than hiding them, and let the trial's own supervised sessions calibrate the home logs. Secondary risks: the app itself becomes a co-intervention (reminders, feedback) that confounds the exercise arm unless Trial Mode is genuinely stripped or randomised as a factor; and NCT03427970 may already be complete, so the timing depends on a successor protocol and a grant cycle the founder does not control.

**Evidence basis:** HIGH confidence (verified in repo): persist.ts writes exercises_completed with no denominator, no per-set timestamps, no provenance; tools.ts log_exercise writes chat self-report into the same field; repeatability.ts implements SEM/MDC95/Bland-Altman with a 5-pair gate; parseProgram.ts ingests physio programmes with source_text; docs/cuhk-approach.md frames adherence measurement as the sole differentiator and names NCT03427970 as primary target. HIGH confidence (confirmed audit findings supplied): the adherence and dose findings (evidence/major, evidence/minor) that no prescribed dose or provenance exists; biomech triage of which form checks are plausible vs theatre; safety_informatics findings that the ongoing safety screen is dead code and that no age gate exists. MEDIUM confidence (from the digest, not independently re-verified here): PSSE trial doses (Schreiber 2015/2016 ~30-45 min/day home plus weekly supervised; Yuan 2025 30 min/day, >=5 days/week, 6 months) and the ">=80% per-protocol" adherence convention - consistent with my clinical recollection but no source was fetched in this run. LOW confidence: current recruitment status of NCT03427970 and whether a successor protocol exists - unverified; the plan should not depend on that specific trial. No numeric claims about trial cost or market size are made because none could be sourced.

**Time to first value:** ~6 weeks to the research-conversation deliverable: adherence event schema, a locked prescription object, a sample pseudonymised export with data dictionary, and a written validation protocol - enough to walk into a CUHK O&T researcher's office with something a statistician can critique. 8-12 weeks to a usable Trial Mode build (ledger, provenance, coaching stripped, export, minors gating). ~3 months after that for the supervised-observation validation pilot that makes "verified adherence" a defensible claim. First trial participant enrolled on the instrument: 6-18 months, governed by ethics approval and the partner's protocol/grant cycle rather than by engineering.


### The Watch Window: a clinician-shared observation ledger for HK's observed-but-untreated cohort
_Angle: hk_system_

**Turn Balance from an adult exercise coach into the between-visit monitoring record for every HK schoolchild who screened positive but is only being "observed" — a per-child ledger of helper-measured ATR at the known apex (gated on the child's own repeatability), height velocity and maturity milestones, brace-window status, and PSSE adherence — that the Student Health Service / Special Assessment Centre or orthopaedic clinic receives as a one-page summary at each 6–12-month review, with exercises demoted to an adjunct module inside it.**

**Why 100x:** Mechanism, not adjectives: (1) Cohort size and structure. If ~2.8% of screened students are referred and ~9% of those need treatment, then ~91% of referrals — roughly 2.5% of all screened students, i.e. ~10x the treated group — are placed in watchful waiting. Nobody owns that interval today: the child is seen every 6–12 months, and between visits the family has no instrument, no record, and no way to know whether to come back early. The product that owns the interval owns the cohort. (2) Buyer changes from individual to institution. An exercise coach is sold one adult at a time; an observation ledger is a pathway component that SHS/SAC or a hospital ortho clinic wants for every observed child, because it answers the one question their 6-monthly visit cannot: what happened in the gap. That converts consumer churn into a per-cohort, per-year institutional relationship (and is exactly the "between-visit monitoring, not screening" position docs/cuhk-approach.md already reaches — but never encodes as product). (3) It is the only cohort where the product's exercise content is actually indicated. SOSORT positions PSSE as first-line specifically for small curves in growing patients — which is the definition of the observed cohort. For adults with fixed curves the coach promises pain/function; for observed adolescents it is the guideline-recommended intervention, delivered where it must happen (home) with the adherence provenance the CUHK pitch depends on. (4) The data asset compounds. Longitudinal home ATR at the apex + height velocity + menarche/Risser + adherence + pain, on thousands of observed children, is the input that progression-prediction work (the "three-year conversation" TOCI line in the CUHK doc) does not have. The ledger makes that dataset a by-product of routine care rather than a study you have to fund. (5) It converts the product's honesty into the differentiator. The whole audit says the product cannot claim curve change; a ledger's job is not to claim change but to bring a repeatability-gated reading, a growth spurt flag, and a brace-window prompt to the clinician on time. "Book your SAC visit sooner — your helper readings moved more than your own noise floor and you grew 4 cm in 5 months" is a claim the instrument CAN support, and it is worth more to a family and a clinic than any exercise.

**Builds on:** - lib/atr/compute.ts: captureAtr, TRUNK_LEVELS, summarise, atrPairsFrom — the phone-inclinometer ATR and the same-day pair collector; needs the audit fixes (apex-from-report as primary site, sweep mode, orientation confirmation, per-level trend, drop 7° band) but the instrument and the repeatability hook exist.
- lib/pose/repeatability.ts: computeRepeatability / buildMdcTable / describeDetectionLimit — the per-user MDC machinery that makes "changed vs noise" an honest statement; raise MIN_PAIRS_FOR_MDC per the biomech finding and it is the ledger's core gate.
- lib/safety/redFlags.ts three-tier triage + condition-free copy style — the vehicle for the new adolescent items (juvenile onset, painful curve, apex hyperkyphosis, brace-window REVIEW flag).
- Physio handoff PDF + Gemini X-ray REPORT parsing (confirm-per-field) — repurpose the report parser to ingest the SAC/orthopaedic letter (Cobb, apex, convex side, Risser) and the PDF to become the clinic-facing "since last visit" summary.
- selectProgram.ts physio_cleared mode — the mandatory path for minors; the ledger's exercise module is physio_cleared only, capped to Tiers 4–5 when no prescription exists.
- Outcomes module (PSFS, pain days) and the adherence event schema proposed in the evidence findings — the adherence half of the ledger.
- docs/cuhk-approach.md — the institutional route (adherence sub-study with CUHK O&T; between-visit ATR positioning) is already written; this feature is what makes that document a product rather than a pitch.

**Needs:** ENGINEERING (~3 months to a pilotable build, given the audit fixes are prerequisite): (a) ship the ATR fixes (apex-keyed primary site, sweep/max mode, orientation confirmation, zero-offset, 4 s median window, per-level trend, MDC gate at ≥10 pairs with conservative bound); (b) new profile fields: date of birth, sex, menarche status/date, self-measured height log (monthly), Risser/Cobb/apex from clinic letter, brace status and prescribed hours, next scheduled review date; (c) a growth-velocity computation (cm/yr from height log) and a deterministic "bring forward your visit" rule set — ATR change > personal MDC at apex, height velocity above a clinician-set threshold during the immature window, new pain, any red flag — that never emits "stable"; (d) guardian consent + child assent flow, minors default to no LLM chat/no Companion nudges, no scan-derived body numbers; (e) a clinic-facing one-page PDF/CSV per child per interval and a clinic dashboard listing observed children by review-due date and open flags; (f) Traditional Chinese throughout (helper is a parent). CLINICAL: a paediatric spine orthopaedic surgeon and a certified PSSE physiotherapist to set the bring-forward thresholds and sign the exercise tiers permitted for minors; a clinical hazard analysis with the false-reassurance hazard named. DATA: a same-day ATR pair at enrolment plus one at every clinic visit (clinician-measured scoliometer alongside) to establish helper-vs-clinician agreement — this is the validation study and it is small. PARTNERSHIPS: one orthopaedic clinic's observation arm (CUHK O&T via the sub-study framing, or a private paediatric spine clinic) as the pilot; the Department of Health SHS is the eventual counterparty but must not be approached with an unvalidated instrument, and the school cohort must never be recruited through schools without DH. REB: parental consent and child assent in Chinese and English, data-minimisation for minors, named escalation clinician.

**Biggest risk:** False reassurance that costs a child the bracing window. ATR–Cobb correlation is only moderate; a curve can progress radiographically while a parent-placed phone at four levels reads flat, and a family holding a ledger of "nothing moved" is exactly the family that skips or defers the SAC visit — the one outcome that makes the product net-harmful. This is compounded by the helper's laterality and level-placement error, and by the institutional risk that a private app sitting alongside the DH pathway is seen as a parallel screener (the comparison the CUHK doc already says you lose). Mitigations must be designed in, not bolted on: the ledger only ever emits "keep your scheduled visit" or "bring it forward"; it never emits reassuring language; the scheduled-review date is a hard field and the product nags toward it; every summary carries "only an X-ray measures your curve"; growth velocity and maturity — not ATR — drive the brace-window prompt. If the team cannot commit to a product that is forbidden to say "stable", it should not build this.

**Evidence basis:** Confidence notes (sources not re-fetched this run; figures from memory and from the supplied digest): HIGH — the HK pathway shape: SHS screens students annually from around Primary 5, refers ATR 5–14° to moiré/periodic re-screen and ATR ≥15° or ≥2 moiré lines to radiograph (Luk et al. 2010, Spine; Fong et al. 2015, Spine J) — exact cut-offs to be verified before copy ships, as the digest already requires. MEDIUM — the ~2.8% referral / ~9% needing treatment figures are as given in the brief; the derived "observed ≈ 10x treated" follows arithmetically and is the load-bearing claim; the absolute cohort size (tens of thousands of HK children under observation at any time) is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a verified number. HIGH — SOSORT 2016 guidelines (Negrini et al. 2018) recommend PSSE for small curves in growing patients and bracing from roughly 20–25° with growth remaining; BrAIST (Weinstein et al. 2013, NEJM) established bracing efficacy, which is why the window matters. MEDIUM — peak height velocity / growth spurt and Risser/menarche as progression predictors are standard (Lonstein & Carlson 1984; Sanders et al.), but I have not verified specific velocity thresholds and the product should take them from its clinical advisors. MEDIUM — helper-measured scoliometer agreement with clinicians is not established (the digest's biomech finding); the enrolment-visit pairing is the proposed fix and is unproven. HIGH (from repo) — docs/cuhk-approach.md already positions ATR as between-visit monitoring and names CUHK NCT03427970/NCT03904914; repeatability and pair-collection code exists in lib/pose/repeatability.ts and lib/atr/compute.ts.

**Time to first value:** First value to a family: at the second same-day ATR pair (enrolment + first repeat), i.e. within the first clinic interval — the child gets a personal noise floor and a "bring this to your visit" summary instead of nothing. First value to a clinic: the first review cycle after pilot start (~6 months), when the clinician receives between-visit summaries for an observation-arm caseload. Build time: ~3 months of engineering after the prerequisite audit fixes (ATR, minors gate, library convention) plus REB approval running in parallel (~3–6 months in HK); realistic first pilot data at 9–12 months, and the institutional (DH/SHS) conversation only after a clinic pilot has shown helper-vs-clinician agreement.

## Independently verified by the session lead (web sources, 2026-09-05)

These were checked outside the auditor/refuter loop and either correct or sharpen the findings above.

- **`frog-in-the-pond-unsourced` — partly wrong, finding stands.** The auditor could not confirm "Frosch am Teich" exists. It does: the Schroth *Frog at the pond* is performed **sitting with legs folded back and heels beside the body**, pelvis position varying by curve pattern (3-curve vs 4-curve), used for clinical over-correction of thoracic curves >40°. The library's version — *supine, knees dropped left, left hand on neck, right hand at 90°* — is **not that exercise**. Conclusion unchanged: cut it or rewrite it as the real thing with a left-thoracic mirror. (Source: Lehnert-Schroth method description, Scoliosis & Spinal Disorders 2011; figure captions from a 2022 Schroth monograph.)
- **`side-plank-absolute-contra` / `side-bridge-test-contradicts-own-contraindication` — strengthened.** Fishman, Groessl & Sherman 2014 (Global Adv Health Med) was an uncontrolled serial case series, n=25. **Sarkisova et al. 2019 (CHLA / Skaggs group, Global Adv Health Med) replicated the protocol with a control arm and found no significant change in primary Cobb angle after 6 months**, concluding the pose "may not be warranted." The convex-side-down rule rests on a study that failed replication; the "wrong side = absolute contraindication" rule has no evidence base at all.
- **`no-bracing-indication-gate` — confirmed thresholds.** SOSORT 2016: PSSE/observation for Cobb <25°; bracing + PSSE for 25–40° in the skeletally immature (Risser 0–3; or >25° with no signs of maturity); >40° surgical referral. The product has no Cobb/maturity stratification and never states it must not substitute for bracing.
- **Side-bridge 0.05 — implementation correct.** McGill's battery specifies the right/left side-bridge ratio should be within 0.05 of 1.0. The biomech auditor's point that this is a *training target, not a detection limit* also stands.
- **The Yuan 2025 comparator is further away than the strategy doc implies.** The "Healbone Intelligent Rehabilitation System" (Jiakangzhongzhi Technology) is a smartphone app **plus IMU wearables plus a clinician portal** (create/modify exercises, monitor progress, view patient data), with therapist-made per-exercise videos; the intervention arm had 1 in-clinic PSSE session then 6 months of remotely supervised home training at 30 min/day, ≥5 days/week. Balance has none of the three components (sensors, clinician portal, therapist-prescribed content). **Action:** soften "your thesis is already proven" in `docs/product-strategy.html` to "the *supervised-PSSE-via-software* thesis is proven; Balance's self-guided mode is not that."
- **New competitor trial.** NCT07341633 — *AI-Based Digital Therapeutics vs Traditional Schroth Exercises for AIS* — Jinan University Guangzhou, started Feb 2026, primary completion Jan 2028. Watch it.
- **Adherence evidence base.** JMIR 2025 scoping review of behaviour-change strategies in digital AIS exercise interventions: 21 studies of 3,267 screened; videoconferencing (n=7) and instructional videos (n=5) dominate. Full text proxy-blocked; BCT breakdown not retrieved.
